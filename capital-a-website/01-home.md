## What We Are

Most M&A firms sell advice. We built something older.

The firms that shaped media, from Allen & Company to the houses behind today's technology deals, ran one model: advise the sector you know, invest in the best of what you see, and host the room where the industry meets. Capital A runs that model for marketing, creative and agency businesses.

Advisory keeps us inside every deal in the market. Investing means we back our judgement with our own capital. Convening, through Agencies.co, our research and our events, means the market comes to us first.
