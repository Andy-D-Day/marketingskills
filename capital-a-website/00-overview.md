# Capital A Website Repositioning
## Working Copy — July 2026

---

### The Positioning Shift

**Old frame:** Tech-enabled M&A firm offering a comprehensive suite of services. A vendor. Asks for work in the first headline.

**New frame:** The investment and advisory house for the agency economy. A principal. States what it is, shows what it has done, and lets qualified people approach.

**Behavioural rule across every page:** Nothing on this site chases. Sell-side lead capture moves entirely to Agencies.co, where the funnel machinery belongs. Capitala.co becomes the institutional layer: the site a founder checks after the first call, the site Mobeus checks before the second meeting, the site Eran checks before writing a cheque. Its job is to make everything else you do more expensive.

---

### Sitemap

**Keep and rewrite:** Home, Our Story, Team, Insights (currently News & Media), Contact.

**Restructure:** The four service pages (Deal Flow, Buy an agency, Sell your agency, Agency Roll-Ups) collapse into two: Advisory and Invest. Sell-side becomes a referral to Agencies.co, not a page. The roll-ups page dies; its replacement is Invest.

**New nav:** Home | Advisory | Invest | Track Record | Insights | Team | Contact

**Delete on sight:** the "virtual roll ups that exit for double the multiple" paragraph.

---

### SEO and Metadata

**Title tag:** Capital A | The investment house for the agency economy

**Meta description:** Capital A advises, invests in and convenes the agency economy. M&A advisory through Agencies.co, principal investment through Agency A. London.

**Note:** The lead-gen keywords (sell your agency, valuations) belong to Agencies.co and should concentrate there. Splitting them across two domains costs both sites authority.

---

### What Is Not on This Site

- No pricing tables (they live on Agencies.co)
- No "book a call" widgets
- No Substance announcement until close
- No "merchant bank" as a label anywhere, per sensitive words rules (the phrase can appear inside written thinking on the Insights page describing the model, never as a claim about the firm)
- No AI Visibility Engine or other products
- No NY or LA addresses unless someone actually sits in them
