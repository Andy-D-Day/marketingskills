## Product Knowledge

### Product 1: Self-Serve Agency Listing (For Sellers)

**Page:** agencies.co/list-your-agency

**What it is:** A self-serve listing on the Agencies.co marketplace that puts your agency in front of qualified buyers — PE firms, strategic acquirers, and holdcos actively looking to buy agencies.

**How it works (4 steps, takes minutes):**

1. Enter your agency website URL. AI scans it and pre-fills your listing with company details, services, team size, and positioning.
2. Review and edit the pre-filled details, add contact information, and confirm whether you're the owner or a broker.
3. Enter your financials (revenue, EBITDA) and upload supporting documents. The platform calculates a suggested valuation range automatically.
4. Choose your plan, sign the listing agreement, and pay via Stripe.

**Pricing:**

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 6-month listing | $2,500 one-time | ~$417/mo, best value |
| Monthly | $699/month | Flexible, cancel anytime |

**What's included:**

- AI-powered self-serve onboarding (listing created in minutes, not weeks)
- Professional marketplace listing visible to the buyer network
- "New Listing" email blast to qualified buyer database
- Adjusted EBITDA calculation
- M&A team support once buyers show interest
- CIM template to download and customise (sellers upload their own branded version)
- AI-powered matching against active buyer mandates

**Target audience:** Agency owners (digital, creative, PR, media, performance marketing, SEO, content) with $500K-$20M revenue who are considering selling but don't want a $12K-$30K advisory retainer. Also brokers representing agency clients.

### Product 2: Buyer Mandate (For Buyers)

**Page:** agencies.co/submit-mandate

**What it is:** A public, anonymised acquisition mandate on the Agencies.co marketplace. It tells agency founders what type of agency you want to buy — without revealing your identity — and the matching engine connects you with relevant agencies automatically.

**How it works (3 steps):**

1. Define basics: target revenue/enterprise value range, geographies, services sought, preferred headcount range.
2. Set preferences: buyer type, target sectors, deal structure preferences (cash, earn-out, equity roll), timeline, revenue model preference.
3. Preview anonymous mandate (published under a generated project name like "Project Blue Hawk"), choose plan, and pay.

**Pricing:**

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 12-month mandate | $4,900 one-time | ~$408/mo, best value |
| Monthly | $499/month | Flexible, cancel anytime |

**What's included:**

- Email alerts when new matches are found or founders register interest

- CRM sync for all leads
- Agency founders register interest directly through the mandate page

**Target audience:** PE firms, strategic acquirers, holdcos, and individual buyers actively acquiring marketing/communications agencies. Typically managing multiple targets or running buy-and-build strategies.

## Key Differentiators (Use in Every Script)

### 1. No Win Fees — Lead With This

This is the single biggest differentiator. Traditional M&A brokers and competing platforms charge 5-15% of the transaction at close. Agencies.co charges a flat listing or mandate fee only. No success fees, no commissions, no percentage of the deal.

**How to frame it:**

- "Other platforms take 5-15% of your deal at close. We charge a flat fee. That's it."
- "On a $5M deal, a typical broker takes $250K-$750K in success fees. With us, you pay $2,500."
- "No win fees. No success fees. No finders fees. Flat fee, full stop."

### 2. Speed and Simplicity

Traditional M&A is slow, expensive, and opaque. Agencies.co is the opposite.

- Listing created in minutes (AI scans your website and pre-fills everything)
- Traditional advisors take weeks to build a comparable profile
- Self-serve means you control the process and timeline

### 3. Low-Barrier Entry

Monthly pricing reduces commitment anxiety.

- Sellers: $699/mo to test the market. Cancel anytime.
- Buyers: $499/mo to test deal flow. Cancel anytime.
- Frame as: "Test the market without a $12K-$30K advisory retainer."

### 4. Privacy and Anonymity

- Seller financials only shared with NDA-signed, qualified buyers
- Buyer mandates published under generated project names (e.g., "Project Blue Hawk")
- You control when and how to reveal yourself

### 5. Upgrade Path

If serious buyers/deals emerge, sellers can upgrade to full M&A advisory support ($2,500 for 10 hours).

## Tone Guidelines

- **Direct and credible.** No hype. Agency owners and PE buyers are sophisticated.
- **Peer-to-peer.** Write as someone who understands M&A, not as a salesperson.
- **Specific over vague.** Use real numbers — "$2,500 flat" beats "affordable pricing."
- **Confident, not pushy.** State the value proposition and let it speak.
- **Professional but conversational.** Contractions are fine. Jargon is fine when the audience uses it (EBITDA, CIM, LOI, earn-out).

## Adaptation Notes

When writing any script, adapt based on:

- **Channel**: LinkedIn is shorter and more conversational. Video is punchier. Podcast reads are warmer and more narrative.
- **Audience segment**: Agency owners respond to "no win fees" and speed. PE buyers respond to "deal flow" and "flat fee economics."
- **Specificity**: Always use real numbers where possible. "$2,500 flat" is stronger than "affordable." "Minutes, not weeks" is stronger than "fast."
- **Social proof**: If available, reference specific metrics — number of listed agencies, buyer inquiries, time to first interest.
