# Post 5 — You're Not an Owner, You're the Highest-Paid Employee

**Audience:** Entrepreneur
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

You built a $5M agency. You still approve every proposal. You're not an owner — you're the highest-paid employee.

I know that's hard to hear. You started this thing from nothing. You closed the first client. You built the team. You earned every dollar of that $5M.

But here's the question that matters: if you stopped working tomorrow, what would happen in 90 days?

If the answer is "the business would be in serious trouble," then you don't own a business. You own a job. A well-paying job with a lot of stress and overhead, but a job nonetheless.

At $3-5M, most agency founders are still the chief salesperson, the chief strategist, and the chief firefighter. They're the person clients call when something goes wrong. They're the person the team escalates every hard decision to. They're the person who closes every deal over $50K.

That's not ownership. That's dependency disguised as leadership.

The shift that changes everything is brutal: you go from "nobody can do it like me" to "I need to build the team that makes me irrelevant."

That feels wrong. It feels like giving up control. But it's the only path to a business that has real enterprise value.

Three hires that change the game:

**Head of Operations.** This person owns delivery, processes, and team management. They're the reason your team stops coming to you with every problem. Average cost: $120-150K. ROI: you get 20+ hours a week back immediately.

**Head of Client Services.** This person owns every client relationship. They become the first call, not you. They manage renewals, upsells, and retention. Average cost: $130-160K. ROI: client retention actually improves because they get a dedicated, senior point of contact instead of a distracted founder.

**Head of Growth.** This person owns pipeline generation and new business. They build the sales engine that doesn't depend on your personal network. Average cost: $140-180K base + commission. ROI: takes 12 months to ramp, but once they do, you have a business that grows without you selling.

Here's the paradox every agency founder has to internalize: the less you're needed, the more your business is worth. A buyer isn't paying for your skills. They're paying for a machine that generates profit without you.

Every hour you spend doing work someone else could do is an hour you're actively reducing the value of your company.

What's the one function you can't let go of? That's your bottleneck.
