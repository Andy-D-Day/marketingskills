# Post 13 — 200 Agency Deals Later, Here's the #1 Red Flag

**Audience:** Investor
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

I've looked at 200+ agency deals in the last 3 years. The #1 red flag has nothing to do with financials.

It's this: the founder can't explain what the agency does in one sentence.

I sit down with founders running $5M+ agencies and ask: "What do you do?" The answer is 90 seconds long, mentions four service lines, three industries, and ends with "...we help brands grow."

When I hear that, I know three things:

1. No positioning. They take whatever walks through the door.
2. No differentiation. They compete on relationships, not a defensible process.
3. No repeatable delivery model. Every engagement is custom, so margins are unpredictable.

The deeper issue: if the founder can't articulate it, clients are buying the founder — not the service. That's the biggest risk in any agency acquisition.

"Full-service" is the worst offender. When I see that on a pitch deck, I read: "We'll do whatever you pay us for." That's freelancing with employees.

The agencies that command 6-8x multiples share one trait: a clear, narrow identity.

"We build paid search campaigns for DTC e-commerce brands doing $5-50M in revenue."

One sentence. Specific service. Defined audience. Clear scope.

Buyers want: "We do X for Y and our process is Z." Everything else is noise.

Can you explain your agency in one sentence? Try it in the comments.
