# Post 1 — Your Agency Isn't Worth What Your Broker Says

**Audience:** Seller
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

Your agency isn't worth 6x revenue. Stop listening to brokers who tell you it is.

I've sat across the table from dozens of agency founders who walked in expecting a $30M valuation on $5M in revenue.

Every single time, I have to be the one to deliver the reality check.

Here's the truth most brokers won't tell you:

Buyers don't pay on revenue multiples. They pay on adjusted EBITDA. And for most agencies under $10M in revenue, those multiples sit between 4x and 8x EBITDA — not revenue.

Let me walk you through a real scenario:

Your agency does $5M in revenue. Solid. You're running at 15% net margins after you pay yourself a market-rate salary. That's $750K in adjusted EBITDA.

At a 4x multiple (which is realistic for most agencies without recurring revenue), that's a $3M valuation. Not $30M. Not even $10M.

$3M.

So why do brokers tell you otherwise? Because they need to win your listing. An inflated projection gets you to sign. Then they spend 18 months "testing the market" while your business stagnates and you burn out from tire-kickers.

Before you talk to a single broker, buyer, or advisor, do this:

1. Calculate your real adjusted EBITDA (net profit + owner salary above market + add-backs)
2. Be honest about client concentration — if one client is 20%+ of revenue, discount your multiple
3. Look at your revenue mix — project-based work gets 3-5x, recurring/retainer gets 5-8x
4. Ask yourself: would this business run without me for 90 days? If not, discount again.

The founders who get the best outcomes are the ones who know their real number before anyone else tells them what it is.
