# Post 8 — Stop Chasing $10M Revenue

**Audience:** Entrepreneur
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

Stop chasing $10M in revenue. A $4M agency with 30% margins is worth more than a $10M agency with 8%.

I meet agency founders every month who are obsessed with hitting a revenue milestone. "Once we hit $10M, we'll be in a different league." "Once we cross $10M, we can start thinking about an exit."

No. This is backwards.

Let me show you the math that most founders never run:

**Agency A:** $4M revenue, 30% EBITDA margins = $1.2M EBITDA.
At a 5-8x multiple, that's a **$6M to $9.6M valuation.**

**Agency B:** $10M revenue, 8% EBITDA margins = $800K EBITDA.
At the same 5-8x multiple, that's a **$4M to $6.4M valuation.**

Read that again. The $4M agency is worth more than the $10M agency. In some scenarios, nearly twice as much.

And here's what the math doesn't capture: the $10M agency with 8% margins is probably a nightmare to run. Razor-thin margins mean every bad month is a crisis. Every client loss is an emergency. The founder is running on fumes trying to manage a 40-person team that generates less profit than a lean shop one-third its size.

So why do founders chase revenue?

**Ego.** $10M sounds impressive at dinner parties. $4M doesn't. But ego doesn't deposit in your bank account at close.

**Wrong advisors.** Too many brokers and consultants use revenue as the headline metric because it's easy to understand. It's also misleading. Revenue without margins is a vanity metric.

**Misunderstanding of how buyers value agencies.** Buyers don't buy revenue. They buy cash flow. Specifically, they buy the present value of your future adjusted EBITDA. Everything else is noise.

If you want to build a genuinely valuable agency, here's the high-margin playbook:

**1. Fire your low-margin clients.** Every agency has 2-3 clients that consume disproportionate resources for mediocre revenue. Cut them. Your team will thank you and your margins will jump overnight.

**2. Raise your prices.** If you haven't raised prices in the last 12 months, you're leaving money on the table. The clients who push back hardest are usually the ones you should fire anyway.

**3. Productize your delivery.** Stop custom-scoping everything. Create 3-4 defined service packages with fixed deliverables, fixed timelines, and fixed pricing. This is how you scale margin, not headcount.

Doing less, better, is the fastest path to a valuable agency.

What's your actual EBITDA margin? Most founders don't know — and that's the first problem.
