# Post 4 — 7 Signs Your Agency Is Worthless Without You

**Audience:** Entrepreneur
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

7 signs your $3M+ agency is actually worth nothing without you.

I review agency financials for a living. And the most common thing I see is a business that looks great on paper but has zero transferable value.

Revenue is healthy. Clients are happy. The founder is making $400K+ a year. But the moment that founder steps away, the whole thing collapses.

Here's how to know if that's you:

**1. Every major client has your personal cell phone number.**
They text you on weekends. They call you when something breaks. The relationship lives in your phone, not in your business. Fix: Assign a dedicated client lead to every account and transition the primary relationship over 90 days.

**2. You approve every proposal before it goes out.**
Nothing leaves the building without your eyes on it. That's not quality control — that's a bottleneck. Fix: Build a proposal template system and train your team to self-approve within defined parameters.

**3. No one else can sell new business.**
You're the rainmaker. If you stop selling, the pipeline dries up in 60 days. Fix: Hire or develop a Head of Growth and give them 12 months to build their own pipeline before you judge results.

**4. Your top talent would leave if you stepped back.**
They're loyal to you, not the company. They joined because of you. They stay because of you. Fix: Build a culture and compensation structure that retains people independent of any single person.

**5. You haven't taken more than 5 consecutive days off in 2+ years.**
Not because you're driven. Because you're afraid of what happens when you're not there. Fix: Take a two-week trip. Seriously. The fires that start will show you exactly what needs to be systemized.

**6. Your financials are a mess — you run everything through the business.**
Personal car. Family vacations. Your kid's tuition. A buyer looks at your P&L and sees chaos, not a business. Fix: Separate personal and business expenses. Run clean books for at least 12 months before going to market.

**7. There's no documented process for anything that matters.**
Onboarding, delivery, QA, reporting — it all lives in people's heads. Fix: Document your top 10 workflows. Start with client onboarding and project delivery. Use SOPs, not tribal knowledge.

Here's the thing most founders don't realize: fixing even 3 of these doubles your enterprise value. Not because your revenue changes, but because a buyer can finally see a business that runs without the founder.

That's what they're paying for. Not your revenue. Your independence from the revenue.

How many of these hit home? Be honest.
