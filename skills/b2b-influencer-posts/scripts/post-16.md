# Post 16 — Agency Valuations Have Peaked

**Audience:** Seller
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

Agency multiples compressed 15-20% from their 2024-2025 highs. If you're thinking about selling in the next 2 years, here's what that means for your timeline.

From 2021 to early 2025, we lived through the greatest agency valuation bubble most of us will ever see. Cheap capital flooded the market. Every PE firm needed a "digital transformation" thesis. Multiples for mid-market agencies hit 8-10x EBITDA — numbers that would have been laughable in 2018.

That era is over.

Interest rates reset the cost of capital. AI disruption is making buyers question whether today's service delivery model will exist in 3 years. And the flood of agencies hitting the market (because every founder heard the same "now is the time to sell" advice) has created a supply-demand imbalance.

Here's what the numbers look like now:

- Agencies that commanded 8-10x in 2024 are closing at 6-7x in 2026
- Sub-$5M revenue agencies are seeing the sharpest compression — from 5-6x down to 3.5-4.5x
- Deal timelines have extended from 4-6 months to 8-12 months on average

But this is not a "bad" market. It's a normalized market.

Agencies with these characteristics are still commanding premium multiples:

1. AI-integrated delivery that reduces headcount per dollar of revenue
2. 70%+ recurring revenue with 12-month average client tenure
3. A leadership team that runs operations without the founder in the room
4. EBITDA margins above 25%

The worst position you can be in right now is waiting. Waiting for multiples to "come back." Waiting for your revenue to hit some magic number. Meanwhile, your business ages. Your best people get poached. Your clients consolidate vendors.

If you're 12-24 months from a potential exit, the preparation window is now. Not Q3. Not next year. Now.

Get your financials cleaned up. Build the management layer. Shift project revenue to retainers. Document your delivery processes. These things take 12+ months to show up in your numbers — and buyers will see right through anything you rushed.

Are you building your agency to sell in the current market or the one you wish existed?
