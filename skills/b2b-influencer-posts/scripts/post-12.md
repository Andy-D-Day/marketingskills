# Post 12 — Client Concentration Will Kill Your Valuation

**Audience:** Seller
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

Agencies with 80%+ revenue from 3 or fewer clients sell for 40-60% less. Here's how to fix client concentration before you go to market.

I worked with an agency doing $6M. Strong margins. Great team. Two clients made up 65% of revenue.

Every buyer flagged it. Offers came in at 3.5-4x EBITDA instead of the 6-7x the owner expected. One buyer said directly: "If either client leaves post-close, I'm underwater in year one."

Here's the threshold you need:
- No single client above 20% of revenue
- Top 5 clients under 50% of total
- Ideally, 20+ active clients with even distribution

I get why founders tolerate concentration. Big clients are comfortable and profitable. But that comfort sinks your valuation.

The 12-month fix:

Months 1-3: Raise prices 15-25% on your largest clients. They pay more or reduce scope — both reduce concentration.

Months 4-8: Pursue mid-market accounts aggressively. Target $8-15K/month clients instead of $50K+ whales.

Months 9-12: Track concentration quarterly using a Herfindahl index. Below 0.10 is healthy. Above 0.15 is a problem.

A diversified book at $4M is worth more than a concentrated book at $6M. Every time.

What % of your revenue comes from your top 3 clients? Drop the number below.
