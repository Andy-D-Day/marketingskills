# Post 6 — PE Is Coming for Marketing Agencies

**Audience:** Investor
**Platform Scripts:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X

---

## LinkedIn Script

Private equity will acquire more marketing agencies in 2026 than any year in history. Here's why.

I've been tracking agency M&A deal flow for over five years. PE-backed acquisitions of digital agencies rose 40% between 2021 and 2023. They jumped another 35% through 2025.

2026 is going to shatter both of those numbers. Five forces are converging at once.

**1. Recurring revenue models have finally matured.**
A decade ago, most agencies ran on project fees. Today, the strongest shops have 60-80% of revenue locked into monthly retainers. That's predictable, compounding cash flow — and PE prices predictability at a premium. Retainer-heavy agencies are pulling 6-8x EBITDA offers while project-based shops struggle to clear 4x.

**2. Digital agencies have the margin profile PE loves.**
A well-run digital agency operates at 25-35% EBITDA margins. Compare that to traditional agencies grinding at 10-15%. Layer in AI reducing delivery costs by 15-20%, and these margins are only expanding. That's operating leverage PE funds dream about.

**3. The market is absurdly fragmented.**
There are roughly 50,000 digital agencies in the US alone. Thousands sit in the $2-10M revenue range with no institutional backing. That's roll-up paradise. Buy five $3M agencies, centralize operations, and you have a $15M platform doing 30%+ margins. We've seen this playbook crush it in dental, HVAC, and veterinary. Agencies are next.

**4. AI is the forcing function.**
Agencies that don't invest in AI-powered workflows within the next 18 months will lose clients to those that do. That investment takes capital. PE provides it. This is accelerating a "buy or die" dynamic — and it's pushing founders who never considered selling to pick up the phone.

**5. First-time PE buyers are flooding the space.**
Agency deal sizes — typically $3M to $20M enterprise value — are accessible for smaller funds, independent sponsors, and family offices. The barrier to entry for buyers has never been lower. More buyers chasing the same pool of quality agencies means multiples are heading up, not down.

**What this means for founders:**
If you run a $3M+ agency with clean financials and retainer-based revenue, you're already on someone's target list. The question isn't whether you'll get approached — it's whether you'll be prepared when they call.

If you run a $3M+ agency, PE firms are already looking at businesses like yours. What trends are you seeing in agency deal flow?
