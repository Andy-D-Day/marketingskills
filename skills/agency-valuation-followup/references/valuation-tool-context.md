# Agencies.co Agency Valuation Tool — Full Context

This reference document contains the complete specification of the Agencies.co Agency Valuation Tool. Load this when you need detailed context for crafting follow-up emails, landing page copy, or sales materials.

## What It Is

A free online tool at **agencies.co/agency-valuation-tool** that calculates what a marketing or communications agency is worth on the open M&A market. It uses AI to scan the agency's website, extract a business profile, and apply real M&A valuation methodology to produce a range and breakdown of value drivers.

## How It Works — 3 Steps

### Step 1: Enter Your Website URL
The AI scans the agency's homepage and key pages (Services, Team, About, Clients) and automatically extracts:
- Agency type
- Services offered
- Sectors served
- Team size
- Client names
- Locations
- Positioning

### Step 2: Confirm Your Financials
The tool pre-fills what it can from the scan. The owner then adds:
- Annual revenue
- Annual EBITDA
- Revenue growth rate
- Percentage of recurring revenue

Takes about 60 seconds.

### Step 3: Get Your Valuation
The tool calculates:
- A low and high estimated market value
- The EBITDA multiple range applied
- A plain-English breakdown of factors pushing the number up or down

## What the Output Looks Like

- **Valuation range:** e.g., "$3.2M — $4.8M"
- **EBITDA multiple range:** e.g., "4.5x — 6.8x"
- **Helping factors** (things increasing the value):
  - "Strong recurring revenue suggests stable, predictable income"
  - "Diversified service offering reduces client concentration risk"
- **Hurting factors** (things reducing the value):
  - "Modest growth rate limits premium multiple potential"
  - "Single-location agency may limit acquirer appetite"
- **Agency profile summary:** type, headcount, services, sectors — all pulled from the website scan

## Valuation Methodology

### Base Multiples
Derived from real agency M&A transaction data, segmented by:

**Agency type:**
- Performance / Digital
- Creative
- PR & Comms
- SEO / Content
- Social Media
- B2B Tech
- Full-service
- Specialist / Niche

**EBITDA tier:**
- Under $1M
- $1M–$3M
- $3M–$5M
- $5M+

### Adjustments Applied For
- Revenue growth rate
- Recurring revenue percentage
- Team depth
- Service diversity
- Margin health

### Final Range
±20% around the calculated midpoint to reflect market variability.

3. **Built on real M&A data.** Not a generic formula — multiples are calibrated by agency type and size band.

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Agency founders and owners — digital, creative, PR, performance, SEO, social, B2B tech — who are curious what their business is worth.

**Motivations vary:**
- Actively thinking about selling
- Approaching retirement
- Dealing with a potential acquirer who reached out
- Simply want to understand the value they've built
- Benchmarking against competitors
- Understanding what buyers look for

**Revenue range:** Broadly $500K–$20M in annual revenue.
