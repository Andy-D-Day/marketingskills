# Agencies.co SEO Blog Strategy: Competing with GoMerge

## Competitive Intelligence: GoMerge.com/learn

### What GoMerge Does

GoMerge is a marketplace for buying and selling online businesses. Their blog at `gomerge.com/learn/` uses a **programmatic content template** strategy: 8 article templates repeated across 18+ business verticals, producing 149+ articles.

### GoMerge's 8 Article Templates

| # | Template Pattern | Example |
|---|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | How to Sell a [Business Type] | How to Sell a Digital Agency |
| 2 | How to Value a [Business Type] | How to Value a Digital Agency Before You Sell |
| 3 | Preparing a [Business Type] for Sale | Preparing Your Digital Agency for a Successful Sale |
| 4 | Exit Strategy for a [Business Type] | Smart Exit Strategies for Digital Agency Owners |
| 5 | What Buyers Look for in a [Business Type] | What Buyers Look for When Acquiring a Digital Agency |
| 6 | Common Mistakes When Selling a [Business Type] | Common Mistakes to Avoid When Selling a Digital Agency |

Some verticals also include a 9th template: "How to Increase [Business Type] Value Before Going to Market."

### GoMerge's Non-Agency Verticals (12)

They also cover: Online Business, Shopify Store, Instagram Business, YouTube Channel, Influencer Business, Online Coaching Business, Creator Business, Membership Website, Digital Product Business, Tech Consulting, Professional Services Firm, Podcast Production Company, Video Production Company.

### Article Characteristics

- **Word count**: ~1,800-2,000 words per article
- **Structure**: Introduction → 6-8 H2 sections → Final Thoughts → Related Resources
- **Depth**: Intermediate — actionable frameworks without deep technical detail
- **Unique content**: Low — templates are clearly formulaic with vertical-specific nouns swapped
- **Internal linking**: Links to 2-3 related articles within the same vertical
- **Schema**: Basic article markup
- **CTAs**: Drive to GoMerge's M&A advisory services

## GoMerge Weakness Analysis

| Dimension | Score (1-5) | Notes |
|-----------|:-----------:|-------|
| **Depth** | 2 | Thin, templated content. Same structure with nouns swapped. |
| **Breadth (agency types)** | 2 | Only 6 agency niches. Many high-value types missing. |
| **Lifecycle coverage** | 1 | Only covers selling/exiting. Nothing on starting, growing, managing, or scaling. |
| **Uniqueness** | 1 | Highly formulaic. Read one vertical, you've read them all. |
| **Authority signals** | 2 | No expert quotes, no original data, no named case studies. |
| **Technical SEO** | 3 | Basic markup, decent internal linking within verticals. |

**Key vulnerability**: GoMerge only covers **one phase** of the agency lifecycle (selling/exiting). This is a massive gap for Agencies.co to exploit.

## Agencies.co Strategy

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Audience alignment**: Agencies.co's audience IS agency owners — GoMerge's audience is business sellers generally
2. **Full lifecycle**: Cover start → grow → manage → scale → sell (5x the content surface)
3. **More verticals**: Cover 20 agency types vs. GoMerge's 6
4. **Deeper content**: 2,500-4,000 words with real frameworks, benchmarks, and examples
5. **Community data**: Leverage Agencies.co community insights for unique angles

## Internal Linking Strategy

### Hub-to-Spoke

Every hub page (`/blog/[vertical]-agency/`) links to all 10 spoke articles for that vertical.

### Spoke-to-Hub

Every spoke article links back to its hub page in the introduction and conclusion.

### Spoke-to-Spoke

Each spoke article links to 2-3 related spokes within the same vertical (e.g., "How to Start" links to "Business Plan" and "Getting Clients").

### Cross-Vertical

Each spoke article links to the same template in 2-3 related verticals (e.g., "How to Start an SEO Agency" links to "How to Start a Marketing Agency" and "How to Start a PPC Agency").

### Hub-to-Hub

Hub pages link to the cross-vertical hub content and to 3-4 related vertical hubs.

### Cross-Vertical Hubs

The 15 cross-vertical hub articles link down to the relevant spoke in every vertical (e.g., "Agency Pricing Models" links to the pricing article for all 20 verticals).

## Differentiation Tactics

### 1. Agency-Specific Benchmarks

Include real numbers wherever possible:

- Average revenue per employee by agency type
- Typical profit margins by agency size and type
- Client acquisition cost benchmarks
- Revenue per client benchmarks
- Common pricing ranges by service and market

### 2. Community Insights

Leverage Agencies.co's community for:

- Founder quotes and stories
- Poll data on agency challenges
- Real examples (anonymized if needed)
- Salary and compensation data

### 3. Actionable Templates

Every article should include at least one:

- Checklist, template, or framework
- Step-by-step process with specific actions
- Example calculations or scenarios

### 4. Consolidation Strategy

Where GoMerge splits thin content across 5 articles (how to sell, prepare for sale, valuation, exit strategy, what buyers want), Agencies.co should create ONE comprehensive guide that covers it all — then rank for all 5 keywords with a single, stronger page.

## SEO Technical Requirements

### On-Page

- Target keyword in: title, H1, URL slug, first 100 words, meta description
- 2-3 secondary keywords woven naturally through H2s and body
- FAQ section at the bottom of each article (targets People Also Ask)
- Table of contents for articles over 2,500 words
- Schema markup on every article

### Schema Markup

- `Article` schema on every blog post
- `BreadcrumbList` schema on every page
- `FAQPage` schema on articles with FAQ sections
- `Organization` schema on hub pages

### Technical

- Fast page load (< 2.5s LCP)
- Mobile-responsive layout
- Proper canonical tags
- XML sitemap with all blog URLs
- Internal links crawlable (no JavaScript-only links)
